# 🌉 Life Bridge

### Connecting Hearts, Supporting Lives

Life Bridge is a platform designed to connect people with services and support through every stage of life.

Our goal is to make it easier for people to discover helpful services related to relationships, marriage, mental wellness, and important life transitions.

## ✨ Features

- 💕 Dating and Compatibility
- 🏨 Date & Stay
- 💍 Marriage Services
- ❤️ Relationship Support
- ⚖️ Divorce & Legal Guidance
- 🧠 Mental Wellness Resources
- 🕊️ Funeral and End-of-Life Services
- 🚨 Emergency Resources

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
